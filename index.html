AI Content Factory

AI Content Factory is a private production tool that its owner runs on their own computer. It creates short educational videos (YouTube Shorts) for the owner's own four YouTube channels: Oddly Explained, Psyche & Sense, Lost & Built and Price Wise Real.

With the owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the tool:

  • confirms which of the owner's YouTube channels is connected;
  • reads the performance statistics of those channels (YouTube Analytics) to show them on a private dashboard;
  • uploads videos to those channels, but only videos the owner has personally reviewed and approved.

The tool is not a public service. Only the owner signs in, and no other users' data is accessed.
