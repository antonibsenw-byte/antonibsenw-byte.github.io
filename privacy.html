Privacy Policy – AI Content Factory

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Who we are

AI Content Factory ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner on the owner's own computer to produce and upload YouTube Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public; the only person who signs in is the owner.

Google and YouTube data the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, it requests these permissions:

  • youtube.readonly – to identify the connected channel (channel ID, title, handle, subscriber count) and to list the channel's own videos with their public details (title, publish date, thumbnail address, duration, and view, like and comment counts; the content of comments is not read).
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read aggregated channel and video reports (views, engaged views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, likes, subscribers gained and lost, audience-retention curves). These reports are totals; they contain no information about individual viewers.
  • youtube.upload – to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved, together with their title, description, tags, privacy setting and scheduled publication time.

How the data is used

The data is used only to run the app for the owner: to make sure each video goes to the correct channel, to upload approved videos, and to show the channels' performance on a private dashboard. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train artificial-intelligence models.

How the data is stored and protected

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. They are never written to files, logs or websites.
  • Channel details and analytics are stored in a local database on the owner's computer. The dashboard that displays them is reachable only from that computer.
  • The app has no server or cloud database of its own. The app never sees the owner's Google password; sign-in happens on Google's own page.

Sharing

Google and YouTube data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. It is sent only to Google itself (for example, when a video is uploaded). It is not passed to artificial-intelligence services or any other third party.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy

By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention, revocation and deletion

YouTube data is kept on the owner's computer only while the app is in use. What happens when access ends:

  • Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. The app's disconnect function does the same for one channel: it asks Google to revoke the sign-in token, deletes the token from the Keychain and removes the stored channel details (ID, title, handle, subscriber count).
  • Analytics and upload records. The local analytics database and the local record of uploaded videos (channel and video IDs and publication times) are not deleted automatically. The owner deletes them manually, including any backup copies, within 30 days after access is revoked or the app stops being used.
  • Videos on YouTube. Videos already uploaded stay on the owner's channels and are managed in YouTube Studio; the app never deletes them.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the app use no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
